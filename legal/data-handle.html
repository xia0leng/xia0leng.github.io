Data Handling Requirements

Last Updated: Nov 1 2026

以下要求适用于小冷（Xiaoleng，Xiaoleng LC， XC，下称“我们”）为评估、合作、审阅、发布准备或其他约定目的，向外部个人或组织（下称“接收方”）提供的资料。资料包括但不限于内部资料、尚未公开的内容、附有使用限制的资料及需要在指定时间之前保密的资料，无论通过文件、邮件、消息、演示、访问权限或其他形式提供
These requirements apply to material provided by Xiaoleng (Xiaoleng, Xiaoleng LC and XC, referred to as "we") to external individuals or organisations ("Recipients") for evaluation, collaboration, review, publication preparation or other agreed purposes. Material includes, without limitation, internal information, unreleased content, material subject to use restrictions and information that must remain confidential until a specified time, whether provided through files, email, messages, presentations, access permissions or other means.

本文件规定接收方如何使用、保护和披露所获资料，不是访客隐私政策。网站使用及隐私另见 条款和隐私声明。当交付说明、邮件确认或协议明确引用本要求，且接收方接受相应条件时，本要求构成资料提供条件的一部分；仅浏览本页面不会使所有公开资料自动成为保密资料。另有有效保密协议或书面约定的，就相应事项优先适用该约定
This document governs Recipients' use, protection and disclosure of supplied material, rather than visitor privacy. Website use and privacy are addressed in the Terms & Privacy Notice. These requirements form part of the supply conditions where delivery notices, email confirmations or agreements expressly reference them and the Recipient accepts those conditions. Merely viewing this page does not make all public material confidential. Valid specific nondisclosure agreements or written terms prevail for their respective subjects.

1- 受保护资料 Protected material

  1. 受保护资料包括标注“内部”“保密”“仅供审阅”“禁止转发”“限时保密”或类似说明的内容，以及根据性质、交付目的和披露情境，接收方合理应当知道不宜公开的资料。由该资料形成且仍揭示其内容的摘录、截图、复制件、摘要、分析和笔记，也受相应限制；没有单独加上保密标记不代表可以任意披露
    Protected material includes content marked internal, confidential, review only, do not forward, embargoed or similarly restricted, and material a Recipient should reasonably understand is non-public from its nature, delivery purpose and disclosure circumstances. Extracts, screenshots, copies, summaries, analyses and notes that still reveal it carry the corresponding restrictions. Absence of a separate confidentiality mark does not permit unrestricted disclosure.
  2. 交付时应尽量明确获准用途、接收范围、公开条件和保密期限。口头或演示中披露的保密内容，可通过后续书面说明确认；性质已明显属于非公开的资料，在确认之前也应妥善保护。限制不清楚或不同说明有冲突时，请先向提供方核实，不应自行将资料公开或扩大使用
    Delivery should identify permitted purposes, recipients, release conditions and confidentiality periods where practicable. Oral or demonstrated disclosures may be confirmed in writing afterwards; clearly non-public material must remain protected meanwhile. Ask the provider to clarify unclear or conflicting restrictions before publication or expanded use.
2- 获准用途与权利保留 Permitted purposes and retained rights
  1. 接收方仅可为交付时约定的目的，在必要范围内查看、使用及复制资料。获准审阅不代表获准发布，获准发布不代表可以转售、再许可或用于其他项目。未经事先书面许可，不得将受保护资料用于无关商业活动、对外宣传、公开展示、训练或建立供其他用途使用的资料库
    Recipients may access, use and copy material only as necessary for the purpose agreed at delivery. Review permission is not publication permission; publication permission is not permission to resell, sublicense or use material on other projects. Prior written permission is required for unrelated commercial use, promotion, public display, training or repositories serving other purposes.
  2. 提供资料不转让所有权、知识产权或其他未明确授予的权利，也不构成必须继续合作、公开发布或提供后续版本的承诺。明确开放或另有许可的资料，依相应许可使用
    Providing material does not transfer ownership, intellectual property rights or any other rights not expressly granted, nor does it constitute a commitment to continue collaboration, publish the material or provide subsequent versions. Expressly open or separately licensed material must be used under the applicable licence.
3- 接收范围与转交 Recipients and onward disclosure
  1. 仅可向事先获准、确有工作需要且承担不低于本要求保护义务的人员披露必要部分。发送给一个人或组织不代表可以转发整个团队、关联组织、客户、群组或其他合作方。未经书面同意，不得向名单之外的主体转交、开放共享链接或另行委托处理
    Disclose only necessary portions to previously authorised people who need them for the task and are bound by obligations at least as protective as these requirements. Delivery to one person or organisation does not authorise circulation to an entire team, affiliates, clients, groups or other partners. Written consent is required for additional recipients, shared-link access or further delegated processing.
  2. 组织接收方应确保其获准人员知悉限制，并在适用法律及约定范围内对其遵守情况负责。人员退出或任务变更时，应及时收回资料及权限。未经许可，不得对外确认非公开的合作关系或以我们的名称和标识暗示背书
    Organisational Recipients must ensure their authorised personnel are aware of the restrictions and remain responsible for their compliance to the extent provided by applicable law and agreements. Promptly recover material and revoke access when personnel leave or tasks change. Without permission, do not publicly confirm non-public collaborative relationships or imply endorsement using our name or marks.
4- 限时保密与解禁 Release embargoes
  1. 标注公开时间的资料，在约定日期、时间及其他条件全部满足前不得对外披露。应以交付说明中明确的时区、版本、渠道和范围为准。只有日期、没有时间或时区，或者约定“收到确认后发布”的，应先取得明确确认，不自行推定已经解禁。需要提前发布的，须取得事先书面同意
    Material with a release embargo must not be disclosed until the agreed date, time and all other conditions are met. Follow the time zone, version, channels and scope specified at delivery. If a date lacks a time or time zone, or release requires confirmation, obtain explicit confirmation rather than assuming expiry. Early release needs prior written consent.
  2. 解禁只解除明确获准公开部分的时间限制。原始文件、未采纳版本、内部往来、个人信息及其他仍受限制的内容，不因部分内容公开或日期到来而同时解禁。其他来源的泄露、传闻、预览或未经授权转载，不代表我们已同意公开
    Embargo expiry removes timing restrictions only for the portions expressly approved for release. Source files, unused versions, internal correspondence, personal data and other restricted content are not automatically released because some content is public or a date has passed. Leaks, rumours, previews and unauthorised reposts do not constitute our permission.
  3. 如需调整已经确认的解禁安排，我们将尽快书面通知并与接收方确认；不能仅通过静默更新本页面追溯延长已接受的期限。接收方如发现误发、时间冲突或可能提前曝光，应暂停相关发布并及时联系核实
    If a confirmed release arrangement needs to change, we will notify the Recipient in writing as soon as possible and confirm the change with them; we cannot retroactively extend an accepted period merely by silently updating this page. If the Recipient discovers misdelivery, conflicting timing or possible early exposure, they should suspend the relevant publication and promptly contact us for clarification.
5- 保密期限与例外 Duration and exceptions
  1. 有明确保密期限或解除条件的，按该约定执行。无明确期限的非公开资料，应在披露时确认期限；确认前继续按保密资料处理，直至我们书面解除、适用例外成立或另行约定。商业秘密在依法仍属于商业秘密期间继续受保护；个人资料及第三方受限资料的保护，不因普通发布限制结束而当然终止
    Express confidentiality periods and release conditions govern. For non-public material without a stated period, confirm the duration at disclosure; pending confirmation, protect it until written release, an applicable exception or a further agreement. Trade secrets remain protected while legally qualifying as such. Protection of personal data and restricted third-party material does not automatically end with an ordinary publication embargo.
  2. 接收方能够以合理记录证明下列情况的，相应信息不受保密限制：在披露前已合法知悉且不负保密义务；并非因违约而已公开或后来公开；从有权提供且不违反保密义务的第三方合法取得；或未利用受保护资料而独立形成。部分信息公开，不代表未公开的组合、细节或附件也已公开
    Confidentiality restrictions do not apply to information the Recipient can demonstrate through reasonable records was lawfully known before disclosure without a confidentiality obligation; was already public or later became public without a breach; was lawfully obtained from an authorised third party without violating a confidentiality obligation; or was independently developed without using protected material. Partial publication does not make undisclosed combinations, details or attachments public.
6- 安全保管与外部工具 Safekeeping and external tools
  1. 接收方应至少采用保护自身同类保密资料的谨慎程度，且不低于合理注意标准。限制访问与下载，使用适当身份验证、安全传输及受控存储；保密标记应随必要副本保留。不得把资料放在公开仓库、无访问限制的网盘或可被搜索引擎索引的位置
    Use at least the care applied to your own comparable confidential information, and no less than reasonable care. Restrict access and downloads and use appropriate authentication, secure transfer and controlled storage. Preserve confidentiality notices on necessary copies. Keep material out of public repositories, unrestricted cloud folders and search-indexable locations.
  2. 未经批准，不得把受保护资料上传至允许无关主体访问、保留或再利用的外部工具、自动化服务或共享环境。使用确有必要的服务前，应确认其权限、保留及删除条件符合交付限制；以工具处理资料不会解除接收方责任
    Do not upload protected material to external tools, automated services or shared environments allowing unrelated access, retention or reuse without approval. Before necessary services are used, confirm that their access, retention and deletion conditions fit the delivery restrictions. Using a tool does not relieve the Recipient of responsibility.
7- 依法披露 Required disclosure
  1. 法院命令、有法律效力的要求或其他适用法律要求披露时，接收方可仅在必要范围内披露。法律允许且切实可行时，应事先通知我们，并合理配合申请保密处理或缩小披露范围；如不得事先通知，则须在允许时告知
    Where disclosure is required by court order, a legally binding demand or other applicable law, the Recipient may disclose only what is necessary. Where lawful and practicable, notify us in advance and reasonably assist efforts to obtain confidential treatment or limit the scope of disclosure. If advance notice is prohibited, you must notify us when permitted.
8- 误发、泄露与补救 Misdelivery, exposure and remediation
  1. 收到明显误发或超出授权的资料时，应停止进一步查看或使用，及时联系核实，不得转发或利用。发现丢失、提前公开、未经授权访问或其他泄露时，应立即采取合理措施控制影响，并尽快向 xln.moe/contact 报告，不等待调查全部完成
    Stop further review or use of clearly misdelivered or unauthorised material and promptly seek clarification; do not forward or exploit it. For loss, early publication, unauthorised access or other exposure, immediately take reasonable containment steps and report promptly to xln.moe/contact without waiting for a complete investigation.
  2. 报告应说明已知资料范围、发生时间、涉及接收者及采取的措施，后续补充核实结果。合理配合撤销链接、收回副本、要求删除或更正已发布内容，并保留必要证据；不得擅自销毁记录或进一步传播泄露内容
    The report should describe the known scope of the material, the time of the incident, the recipients involved and the measures taken, with verified findings provided as they become available. Reasonably assist revoking links, recovering copies and requesting deletion or correction of published material, while preserving necessary evidence. Do not improperly destroy records or further circulate exposed content.
9- 返还、删除与继续保护 Return, deletion and continuing protection
  1. 目的完成、接收权限终止或收到符合约定的返还删除要求后，应及时停止使用，并按要求返还或安全删除原件及仍包含受保护内容的副本、摘录和笔记，撤销共享与访问权限；合理请求时书面确认完成情况。保密期限届满本身不意味着接收方取得其他使用权
    When the purpose is complete, access authorisation ends or a return or deletion request consistent with the agreement is received, promptly stop using the material and, as requested, return or securely delete the originals and any copies, extracts and notes that still contain protected content, and revoke sharing and access permissions. Confirm completion in writing on reasonable request. Expiry of confidentiality alone does not grant the Recipient any other usage rights.
  2. 法律要求留存的记录，或正常备份中无法合理单独删除的副本，可仅在必要范围和期限内保留，限制访问、不作日常使用并继续保密；备份按既定周期清除。返还、删除或合作结束，不免除此前违约责任及对合法留存资料的继续保护义务
    Legally required records and copies not reasonably separable from routine backups may be retained only as necessary, with restricted access, no routine use and continued confidentiality. Expire backups on established cycles. Return, deletion or the end of collaboration does not excuse earlier breaches or continuing protection of lawfully retained material.

    Unless expressly promised in writing or required by law, material is supplied in its delivered state without guarantees of completeness, accuracy, fitness for a particular purpose or continued updates. Recipients should assess it for the permitted purpose. Content may change or never be released. These statements do not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or relieve either party of obligations it has expressly assumed.

    Unauthorised disclosure may cause harm not adequately remedied by money alone. For actual or threatened breach, we may seek cessation, protective measures or other remedies under applicable law and valid agreements, and recover losses for which liability is established. This provision does not predetermine court decisions or impose automatic penalties.

    Updates do not retroactively expand restrictions on previous deliveries or rewrite accepted confidentiality arrangements. Chinese and English are intended to match; applicable law and valid specific agreements govern interpretation and enforcement.
